## What this work demonstrates

- **Operational risk framing:** define the action, failure mode, consequence, control gap, owner, and rollback question.
- **Source discipline:** separate verified evidence, inference, stale information, unsupported claims, and unknowns.
- **Judgment-interface design:** separate evidence direction (`+ / 0 / -`) from action-risk signal (Green / Yellow / Red).
- **Control-gap analysis:** compare expected control with evidence of actual operation.
- **Temporal validity:** recognize that prior assurance may become stale when the operating state materially changes.
- **Operational resilience:** distinguish temporary containment from restored capability; define fallback, recovery owner, stop trigger, and restart criteria.
- **Third-party risk:** review dependency, access, evidence, conditions, exceptions, and material-change monitoring triggers.
- **AI governance and evaluation:** perform claim-level trace, source-currency review, deployment-boundary review, metric-goal alignment, uncertainty preservation, and consequential-use gating.
- **Management communication:** produce concise decision memos, escalation briefs, control-gap logs, risk reviews, and reassessment records.
- **Human accountability:** keep approval and responsibility with an accountable human owner.
