## What this work demonstrates

- **Operational risk framing:** converting an unclear situation into an explicit action, failure mode, consequence, control gap, owner, and rollback question;
- **Human-in-the-loop control design:** keeping final accountability with the human user rather than transferring it to AI;
- **Source discipline:** separating verified evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, stale information, and decision-changing unknowns;
- **Judgment-interface design:** separating evidence direction (`+ / 0 / -`) from action-risk signals (Green / Yellow / Red);
- **Uncertainty calibration:** preserving an unresolved state instead of forcing incomplete evidence into false approval or rejection;
- **Control-gap analysis:** comparing expected control with evidence of actual operation;
- **Operational-resilience thinking:** identifying dependency, containment, fallback, restart, and closure conditions;
- **Third-party risk thinking:** classifying dependency and access, requesting proportional evidence, designing conditions and exceptions, and defining monitoring triggers;
- **AI-assisted decision support:** using AI for extraction and organization while preserving source authority and human approval;
- **Compact decision communication:** presenting conclusion, decisive evidence, unknowns, reversal conditions, decision required, and one safe next action;
- **Public-safe technical writing:** documenting a framework clearly while protecting private cases, sensitive data, and proprietary reasoning assets.
