# For Recruiters and Hiring Managers

This repository is a public portfolio artifact demonstrating how I turn ambiguous, high-variance situations into structured judgment and risk-control frameworks.

## What this work demonstrates

- **Operational risk framing:** converting an unclear situation into an explicit next action, consequence, boundary, and rollback question;
- **Human-in-the-loop control design:** keeping final accountability with the human user rather than transferring it to AI;
- **Source discipline:** separating verified evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, and decision-changing information;
- **Risk-boundary design:** identifying when cost is rising, reversibility is shrinking, or execution should pause;
- **AI-assisted decision support:** using AI as an organizer and analysis aid rather than an autonomous executor;
- **Public-safe technical writing:** documenting a framework clearly while protecting private cases, sensitive data, and proprietary reasoning assets.

## Relevant role areas

This work is most relevant to roles involving:

- operational risk and controls;
- governance, risk, and compliance;
- AI governance, evaluation, or safety operations;
- decision-support systems;
- technical operations and issue escalation;
- process documentation and control design.

## Public showcase cases

Two public-safe composite cases demonstrate how the framework works in practice:

1. [`examples/01-source-drift-procurement.md`](examples/01-source-drift-procurement.md) — source conflict, master-data risk, procurement consequences, and reversible approval design;
2. [`examples/02-unverified-change-release.md`](examples/02-unverified-change-release.md) — incomplete validation, release risk, escalation, rollback, and the Human Final Gate.

These examples are deliberately anonymized and generalized. They demonstrate the control pattern without publishing a real organization, person, incident record, drawing, specification, or private calibration chain.

## Important scope note

This repository is a public scaffold and documentation artifact. It is not presented as a production deployment, autonomous agent, regulated professional tool, or complete private engine.

The public material is intentionally limited to what is needed to demonstrate structured judgment, control design, source discipline, and safety boundaries.
