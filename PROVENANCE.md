## Originality and provenance defense

The public provenance record uses a layered defense against later source displacement:

1. **Public Priority Anchor** — stable names, dates, contribution boundaries and canonical artifacts.
2. **Private Evidence Ledger** — fuller origin/development evidence remains outside the public repository.
3. **Derivation Graph** — later work is classified as prior art, adjacent work, independent convergence, derived work, later formalization or reality validation rather than silently replacing the origin node.
4. **Citation Spine** — later revisions link back to canonical dated artifacts.
5. **Claim Boundary** — each originality statement separates what is claimed here, what pre-existed, and what remains uncertain.
6. **Supersession without erasure** — newer versions may supersede wording or implementation without deleting the dated origin chain.

The goal is not to prevent later researchers from producing better theory or terminology. The goal is to keep the original source recoverable and make later reframing auditable.
