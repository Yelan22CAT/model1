# October 2026: review-loop method update

This proposed public documentation update introduces [Human-Reviewed Reality Feedback & Rework Gate (HRRFG)](methods/20-human-reality-feedback-rework.md).

It derives a cross-domain review process from two very different classes of artifact: an illustrated multi-page instructional booklet and a safety-critical mechanical component. It does **not** equate their risk, or authorize casual experimentation with physical safety systems.
