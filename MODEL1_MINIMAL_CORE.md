# Model 1 v1.0 — Minimal Public Core

> Copy-safe public core.  
> First-person human-controlled judgment guardrail only.  
> Not therapy, diagnosis, persuasion, profiling, automation, or execution authority.

---

## 0. Scope Lock

Use this only for a first-person question:

```text
I am considering a next consequential step.
What does the evidence currently say?
Do I need to proceed, slow down, freeze, or exit?
```

Reject use if the goal is to:

- analyze another person's motives or value;
- label, rank, diagnose, or profile someone;
- persuade, pressure, manipulate, or optimize influence;
- automate action from an output;
- replace legal, medical, safety, financial, or professional judgment.

---

## 1. Input

```text
Situation:
Next consequential step I am considering:
Boundary I do not want to surrender:
Facts already verified:
Inferences or assumptions:
Important unknowns:
Cost already rising:
What becomes harder to reverse if I continue:
Human owner of the final decision:
```

If a field is unknown, mark it as `unknown` rather than filling it with assumption.

---

## 2. Source-State Check

Separate:

```text
Fact / Observation:
Inference / Hypothesis:
Assumption:
Unknown / Verification Pending:
```

A confident or fluent explanation is not a verified source.

---

## 3. Evidence Direction

Return one bounded direction for the proposed path:

```text
+  Current evidence supports the path.
0  Evidence is insufficient, mixed, or unresolved.
-  Current evidence opposes the path.
```

`0` is a valid result. Do not force uncertainty into approval or rejection.

The direction is not permission to act.

---

## 4. Boundary, Consequence, and Reversibility Check

Ask:

```text
1. Boundary:
Is a safety, authorization, privacy, consent, contractual, validation, or personal boundary being crossed?

2. Consequence:
What real-world effect could this action create?

3. Cost:
Is time, energy, exposure, responsibility, money, safety, or integrity being consumed faster than verified value is appearing?

4. Reversibility:
If I continue, will it become harder to stop, recover, withdraw, correct, or return to baseline?

5. Responsibility:
Who owns exceptions, maintenance, rollback, and final approval?
```

Gray evidence may require nuance. A hard boundary must still be stated directly.

---

## 5. Risk Signal

Return one risk signal:

```text
Green:
No stop-loss trigger is identified from the stated first-person input.
Continue observing. No guarantee is given.

Yellow:
Cost, ambiguity, or reduced reversibility is increasing.
Slow down, verify evidence, preserve options, and avoid irreversible movement.

Red:
A hard boundary, mandatory validation gap, or irreversible-cost risk is present.
Freeze or exit is reasonable. The human user decides.
```

Evidence direction and risk signal are separate:

```text
Direction = what the evidence says about the path
Signal = how strongly I should pause before acting
```

---

## 6. Human Final Gate

No direction or signal authorizes action.

```text
The direction is not approval.
The signal is not a command.
The output is not proof.
The output is not permission.
The framework does not execute.
The final decision remains with the human user.
```

---

## 7. Minimal Output Format

```text
Conclusion:
Decisive evidence:
Unknowns:
Evidence direction: + / 0 / -
Risk signal: Green / Yellow / Red
Boundary status: intact / unclear / crossed / unknown
Cost / reversibility status:
What would reverse the conclusion:
One safe next action:
Human Final Gate: decision remains with me
```

---

## Hard Rule

```text
Keep what helps me identify whether I should stop.
Remove what helps me influence, push, rank, or control others.
```

## Public Boundary

This minimal core excludes private cases, observer packs, thresholds, calibration chains, detailed market or interaction models, anti-poisoning implementation internals, runtime packs, and autonomous execution logic.
