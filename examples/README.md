# Public Showcase Cases

These cases demonstrate how Model 1 structures operational uncertainty without publishing private records or proprietary reasoning assets.

They are public-safe composites. They are not transcripts of single real events.

## Cases

1. [`01-source-drift-procurement.md`](01-source-drift-procurement.md) — conflicting source records, procurement risk, evidence reconciliation, and reversible approval design.
2. [`02-unverified-change-release.md`](02-unverified-change-release.md) — incomplete technical validation, release risk, escalation, rollback, and the Human Final Gate.

## What to look for

Each case follows the same visible structure:

- situation;
- proposed next action;
- known facts;
- assumptions;
- risk decomposition;
- reversibility;
- review signal;
- recommended control response;
- Human Final Gate;
- public boundary.

The examples demonstrate the interface and reasoning discipline. They do not disclose complete private calibration logic, real identities, organizations, internal records, or proprietary technical details.
