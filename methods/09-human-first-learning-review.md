# Human-First Learning and After-Action AI Review

**Type:** public educational/workflow design example. **Status:** design, not proof of a learning intervention's causal effectiveness or a running application. Last reviewed: 2026-09-19.

A polished answer can be correct while its recipient cannot explain, independently reconstruct or transfer it. When the purpose is learning and the task is low risk, the review should preserve an initial human attempt and use AI as a later evidence and counterexample assistant rather than an automatic replacement for thinking.

## Suggested sequence

```text
Classify task and risk
 -> Human records an initial hypothesis, reasons and unknowns
 -> Independent attempt / reversible test / dialogue with peers
 -> Record observed result and omissions before consulting AI
 -> AI-assisted audit: sources, factual errors, counterexamples, constraints
 -> Human rewrites understanding without copying the generated answer
 -> Later independent attempt on a related but different problem
 -> Evaluate transfer and actual outcome; revise or stop
```

The key measurement is not merely whether a one-time AI response looks good: can the human later identify evidence, explain why a decision was appropriate, notice an exception and complete a related task without the same assistance?

## Assistance boundary

This is not a rule to withhold expert advice. For safety-critical work, medical/legal matters, inaccessible information, irreversible changes, or tight operational deadlines, seeking reliable qualified help **before** acting is often necessary. Graduated hints, worked examples or a full answer may be appropriate according to context. Deliberate humiliation or extreme stress is not evidence-based 'productive friction'.

AI-produced explanations are candidate material until sources are checked. A verified correct answer is not proof of learning; a user's self-report of improvement is not controlled evidence of causality. Keep a dated, minimal record of initial attempt, feedback, later transfer and exceptions, with permission and privacy checks when personal records are involved.
