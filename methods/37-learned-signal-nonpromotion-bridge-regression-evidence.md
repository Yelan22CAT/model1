# 37 — Learned-Signal Non-Promotion & Archived Bridge Regression Integrity

**Status:** User-authorized **method-level Full-Push / Not Frozen** (2026-11-22). Public-safe research/evaluation method. **Not a production Bridge protocol, trusted attestation infrastructure, a model weight update, or proof of a deployed system.**

## Problem
An AI or learned small-model output can be formatted into a strict semantic record, assigned confidence, or passed through a parser. These steps do not make the content a reliable external fact, a human-authorized decision or observed successful effect. Even large synthetic test suites may miss external signer compromise and concurrency/restore failure.

## Evidence-boundary decision procedure

`Input provenance → Typed model / source claim → Candidate syntax validation → Human-authored or verified semantic context → Independent authenticity and witness check → Explicit authorization → Durable effect validation → Human Final`.

1. **Do not promote learned signals into evidence.** Training labels, probabilities, logits, F1 scores, generated explanations and semantic interpretations remain model-originated claims until independently supported. Do not infer private reasoning or real-world truth from output scores.
2. **Keep narrative interpretation distinct from reality.** A narrator's beliefs, emotions, dreams, metaphors and recollected promises are not automatically external events, verified motives or completed actions.
3. **Typed text is not a signer.** `audited:`-looking strings, self-attested `verified` fields and matching test fixtures are not independent proof. Genuine source/authentication roots must be outside the untrusted model and parser.
4. **Treat advisory readiness as non-executable.** Missing fields, nonfinite numbers, unauthenticated human readiness, stale state and unverified effect references must HOLD. Even successful candidate advisory verdicts must not grant `may_act`.
5. **Preserve negative evidence across regression reports.** Hash exact source, record commands and exit/timeout separately, distinguish directed tests, mutations, fuzz and real field evidence; never turn a timed-out suite into a PASS based on other results or earlier logs.
6. **Avoid an unnecessary autonomous architecture.** Use existing deterministic policy/effect gates for scope and authority; a candidate typed message does not install new trust or control planes.
