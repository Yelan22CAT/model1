# 26 — Human-Readable Review and Experience-Evidence Boundaries

**Status:** Public-safe method-level Full-Push / Not Frozen (2026-11-08). **Scope:** Review methodology, not a platform endorsement.

## The interface is not the outcome

Natural-language generation, drag-and-drop configuration, and code are different authoring surfaces. A readable screen may improve comprehension but does not by itself demonstrate correct semantics, authorized execution, safe rollback or successful real-world effects.

`Human objective → visible candidate configuration → reviewable meaning and permission boundaries → independent execution/effect validation → authorized deployment decision`.

Review the same seven plain-language questions regardless of authoring platform: intended result, actual permissions, unknowns, wrong-outcome risks, current running revision, stopping/recovery plan and independently observable test results.

**Readable configuration ≠ correct semantics ≠ verified effect ≠ approved deployment.** A database rollback may not reverse external API, payment, email or device side effects.

## Evidence transfer between settings

For field observation, manufacturer presentations or professional experience, distinguish:

1. A public diagram/product or capability claim.
2. What was physically observed and under which conditions.
3. A person's self-reported earlier work or practical skill.
4. Competence specifically demonstrated or independently verified on the observed product.

Do not silently convert a trade-show observation into certification, product-specific operating experience or supplier partnership.

## Source and reuse boundaries

Data providers and language-platform brochures can motivate a low-resource or multilingual evaluation plan, not certify linguistic coverage. Audit dataset consent, locality, dialect, code-switching, third-party rights, allowed redistribution and actual validated tasks. Prefer minimal sufficient workflows before installing a proprietary platform or adding an agent.

This is a scoped extension to existing human-first comprehension, reality calibration, risk-proportional validation and resume/skill evidence mapping. It publishes no private occupational narrative, participant identity, screenshots, training material or proprietary execution logic.
