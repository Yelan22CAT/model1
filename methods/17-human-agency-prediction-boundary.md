# Human agency and prediction boundaries

**Status:** Public-safe conceptual method, 2026-10-14; not a validated forecasting product.

AI can predict some behavior distributions in bounded tasks; it cannot be assumed to know a person's next decision, private intent or new abstraction. Prediction, explanation and authority are distinct.

## Claim typing
Classify a statement before evaluating it:
- description of a prior pattern;
- population-level probabilistic forecast;
- individual-level conditional prediction;
- proposed causal mechanism;
- new/creative abstraction or out-of-distribution extrapolation;
- adaptive or adversarial response.

Do not turn population behavior into an individual's presumed destiny.

## Evidence gate
1. Obtain the **original, dated forecast** and its source, not only a retrospective claim of success.
2. Bind target, outcome, horizon, falsification rule and a relevant baseline.
3. Check selection bias, repeated/copying sources, changed incentives, data contamination, missing outcomes and distribution drift.
4. Compare actual results with forecasts using suitable calibration or proper scoring rules **only when** sufficient data is available; otherwise abstain.
5. Separate observable behavior from inferred motivation, individual identity, and alleged causal understanding.
6. Respect privacy: ability to infer a sensitive attribute or motive is not permission to profile or take action.

## Decision language
Use conditional scenarios, uncertainty and explicit verification-pending states. A successful prediction does not authorize surveillance, coercion or irreversible action. Human participants may revise their goals; the system must adapt its hypotheses rather than freeze personal labels.

## Research context
The 2025 Nature paper [“A foundation model to predict and capture human cognition”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09215-4) is evidence of predictive capacity within evaluated experimental tasks, not proof of universal mind reading. Published criticism cautions against confusing predictive fit with mechanism understanding.

**Boundary:** This public method includes no private person records, behavioral profiles, personal prediction dossiers or unpublished calibration data.
